1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

1.1 The organisation is committed to supporting flexible working arrangements
    where these are compatible with business requirements. This policy sets
    out the framework for requesting, considering, and implementing flexible
    working arrangements.

1.2 This policy applies to all employees who have completed their probationary
    period. The statutory right to request flexible working applies from day
    one of employment, in accordance with the Employment Relations (Flexible
    Working) Act 2023.

2. TYPES OF FLEXIBLE WORKING AVAILABLE

2.1 The following flexible working arrangements may be considered:
    (a) Part-time working: reduction in contracted hours
    (b) Compressed hours: full-time hours over fewer days
    (c) Flexitime: variation in start and finish times within core hours
    (d) Remote or hybrid working: working from a location other than the
        primary workplace, subject to role requirements
    (e) Job sharing: two employees sharing one role
    (f) Term-time working: working only during academic term periods
    (g) Annualised hours: hours contracted annually with flexible weekly patterns

3. APPLICATION PROCEDURE

3.1 Employees wishing to request a change to their working arrangements must
    submit a completed Flexible Working Request Form to their line manager and
    a copy to HR.

3.2 The organisation will arrange a meeting to discuss the request within
    28 days of receipt. The employee may be accompanied by a trade union
    representative or colleague.

3.3 A written decision will be issued within 2 months of the date of the
    request, unless an extension is agreed with the employee.

3.4 Employees may make up to two statutory requests in any 12-month period.

4. GROUNDS FOR REFUSAL

4.1 A request may be refused on one or more of the following business grounds:
    (a) The burden of additional costs
    (b) Detrimental effect on ability to meet customer demand
    (c) Inability to reorganise work among existing staff
    (d) Inability to recruit additional staff
    (e) Detrimental impact on quality or performance
    (f) Insufficiency of work during the periods proposed
    (g) Planned structural changes

4.2 A request may not be refused on the grounds of the employee's protected
    characteristic. Any perceived link between a refusal and a protected
    characteristic must be documented and escalated to HR.

5. TRIAL PERIODS

5.1 Where both parties agree, a flexible working arrangement may be
    implemented on a trial basis for an agreed period (minimum 3 months).

5.2 At the end of the trial, both parties will review the arrangement.
    Continuation is subject to the arrangement having met business requirements.

6. HOME WORKING — HEALTH, SAFETY, AND DATA SECURITY

6.1 Employees working from home remain subject to the organisation's Health
    and Safety Policy and must ensure their workspace meets the minimum
    requirements set out in the Home Working Safety Checklist.

6.2 Employees must not process or store the organisation's confidential
    information on personal devices unless explicitly authorised.

6.3 A home working risk assessment must be completed and returned to HR
    before any permanent home working arrangement commences.

7. POLICY REVIEW

7.1 This policy will be reviewed annually by the HR Director.
