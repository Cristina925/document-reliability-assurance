1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This policy governs the assessment, onboarding, monitoring, and
    offboarding of all suppliers engaged by the organisation to provide
    goods or services. It applies to all procurement activities regardless
    of value.

1.2 The policy is designed to ensure that:
    (a) Suppliers meet the organisation's standards for financial stability,
        legal compliance, and ethical conduct;
    (b) Third-party risks are identified, assessed, and managed before
        engagement commences;
    (c) Ongoing supplier performance and risk posture are monitored;
    (d) The organisation's contractual and regulatory obligations are
        fulfilled throughout the supplier lifecycle.

2. DUE DILIGENCE TIERS

2.1 The level of due diligence required is proportionate to the risk
    profile of the engagement, determined by spend, criticality, and
    data-sharing requirements.

Tier 1 — Standard (spend < GBP 10,000 per year, no personal data shared):
    Minimum requirements: confirmation of legal entity status; self-declaration
    of no sanctions exposure; Modern Slavery Act compliance statement.

Tier 2 — Enhanced (spend GBP 10,000–250,000 per year or personal data shared):
    Requirements: Tier 1 plus financial health check; cyber security
    questionnaire; evidence of insurance; review of data processing agreement.

Tier 3 — Critical (spend > GBP 250,000 per year or business-critical supply):
    Requirements: Tier 2 plus on-site audit (or remote equivalent); business
    continuity plan review; penetration test summary; reference checks;
    board-level approval.

3. SANCTIONS AND FINANCIAL CRIME CHECKS

3.1 All new suppliers must be screened against relevant sanctions lists
    (OFAC, HM Treasury, EU, UN) before initial payment is made. Screening
    must be repeated annually and upon any material change to supplier details.

3.2 Screening must be conducted using an approved third-party screening tool.
    Results must be recorded in the supplier management system.

3.3 Any positive match must be escalated immediately to the Legal and
    Compliance function. No payments may be made pending review.

4. DATA PROTECTION REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Where a supplier will process personal data on behalf of the organisation,
    a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) compliant with UK GDPR Article 28 must
    be in place before processing commences.

4.2 Suppliers processing personal data classified as special category under
    Article 9 UK GDPR require enhanced data protection due diligence,
    regardless of spend tier.

5. ONGOING MONITORING

5.1 All Tier 3 suppliers are subject to annual performance reviews against
    agreed KPIs. All Tier 2 suppliers are reviewed every two years.

5.2 Significant adverse events (supplier insolvency, regulatory action,
    significant data breach) must be escalated to the Procurement Director
    within one business day of becoming known.

6. POLICY OWNERSHIP AND REVIEW

6.1 This policy is owned by the Director of Procurement and is reviewed
    annually. The next review is due by 28 February 2025.
