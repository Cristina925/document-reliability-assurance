1. PURPOSE, SCOPE, AND AUTHORITY

1.1 This framework establishes the organisation's information security policy
    hierarchy and defines the mandatory requirements applicable to all
    information assets, systems, personnel, contractors, and third-party
    partners who handle, process, or store information on behalf of the
    organisation.

1.2 This framework is issued under the authority of the Chief Information
    Security Officer (CISO) and has been approved by the Executive Committee.
    It supersedes the Information Security Policy v1.2 dated March 2022.

1.3 Compliance with this framework is mandatory for all in-scope parties.
    Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action, contract termination,
    or regulatory referral as appropriate.

2. POLICY HIERARCHY

2.1 This framework establishes four tiers of policy documentation:
    Tier 1 — Framework (this document): mandatory principles and obligations
    Tier 2 — Domain Policies: detailed requirements per domain (see §6)
    Tier 3 — Standards: technical and procedural specifications
    Tier 4 — Guidelines: advisory best practice and operational guidance

2.2 Where a conflict exists between tiers, the higher tier takes precedence.

3. MANDATORY SECURITY PRINCIPLES

3.1 Confidentiality: Information must be accessible only to those with a
    legitimate need and appropriate authorisation.

3.2 Integrity: Information must be accurate, complete, and protected from
    unauthorised modification.

3.3 Availability: Information and systems must be available to authorised
    users when needed, subject to agreed service levels.

3.4 Accountability: All access to information assets must be attributable to
    identified individuals and must be logged where technically feasible.

3.5 Proportionality: Controls must be proportionate to the sensitivity of the
    information and the risk of its compromise.

4. ACCESS CONTROL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Role-based access control (RBAC) is mandatory for all systems handling
    RESTRICTED or above classified information.

4.2 Privileged accounts (administrator, root, service accounts) must not be
    used for routine work. Privileged access must be time-limited and logged.

4.3 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for:
    (a) all remote access to organisation systems;
    (b) access to systems classified CONFIDENTIAL or above;
    (c) all administrative and privileged accounts;
    (d) access to financial and HR data systems.

4.4 Access reviews for critical systems must be conducted at least quarterly.
    All other systems require annual access reviews.

5. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT

5.1 All security incidents — including suspected incidents — must be reported
    to the Security Operations Centre within one hour of discovery.

5.2 Personal data breaches must be assessed within 72 hours for notification
    obligations under UK GDPR Article 33. The Data Protection Officer must be
    notified immediately upon identification of a potential personal data breach.

5.3 A post-incident review must be completed within 10 working days of
    incident closure for all incidents rated P1 or P2.

5.4 The organisation maintains a cyber incident response plan (CIRP-001) which
    is tested annually through tabletop exercises.

6. DOMAIN POLICY REGISTER

6.1 The following Tier 2 domain policies are in force under this framework:
    ISP-02  Asset Management Policy
    ISP-03  Cryptography Policy
    ISP-04  Physical and Environmental Security Policy
    ISP-05  Operations Security Policy
    ISP-06  Communications Security Policy
    ISP-07  Supplier Relationship Security Policy
    ISP-08  Business Continuity and Resilience Policy
    ISP-09  Compliance and Audit Policy

6.2 Domain policies must be reviewed at least every two years or following
    a material change to the threat landscape or regulatory environment.

7. EXCEPTIONS

7.1 Exceptions to this framework may be granted by the CISO for a defined
    period with documented risk acceptance from the relevant business owner.

7.2 Exceptions must be reviewed at least annually. Expired exceptions that
    have not been renewed are void and the relevant control is immediately
    reinstated.

8. REVIEW SCHEDULE
