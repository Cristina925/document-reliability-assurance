1. SCOPE AND OBJECTIVES

This quarterly review was conducted by the Internal Audit function for the
period 1 July to 30 September 2024. The review assessed the adequacy of
financial controls across the Accounts Payable, Treasury Management, and
Expense Reimbursement functions.

2. KEY FINDINGS

2.1 Accounts Payable
Finding: 4.7% of invoices processed in the quarter were approved by a single
signatory for amounts exceeding the GBP 25,000 dual-authorisation threshold.
This represents 23 invoices with a total value of GBP 812,400.
Risk rating: HIGH
Recommendation: Enforce dual-authorisation controls in the ERP system for
all transactions above the defined threshold. Controls should prevent single-
signatory approval rather than relying on procedural compliance alone.
Management response: The Finance Director has agreed to implement a system
control by 31 October 2024.

2.2 Treasury Management
Finding: The organisation's cash forecasting model was not updated following
a change in the payment terms for its three largest suppliers in August 2024.
This resulted in a short-term liquidity shortfall of GBP 340,000 for 6 days
in September 2024, which was covered by the revolving credit facility.
Risk rating: MEDIUM
Recommendation: Establish a formal process for updating the cash forecasting
model when material changes to payment terms occur.
Management response: A formal change-notification procedure will be documented
by Finance Operations by 15 November 2024.

2.3 Expense Reimbursement
Finding: 12 expense claims submitted during the quarter were processed without
receipts, in breach of the Expenses Policy (Finance Policy FP-003). The
aggregate value of unsupported claims was GBP 4,210.
Risk rating: LOW
Recommendation: Finance Operations to remind claimants of the policy
requirement and to implement a validation step in the claim submission process.
Management response: Agreed. To be implemented before Q4 expense cycle.

3. PRIOR PERIOD ACTIONS

3.1 Three actions from the Q2 2024 review are confirmed as closed:
    (a) Bank reconciliation frequency increased to weekly (completed August 2024)
    (b) Access controls for treasury management system reviewed and updated
    (c) Supplier master data cleanse completed

3.2 One action from Q2 remains open:
    The review of delegated financial authorities has been delayed due to the
    ongoing Finance Director recruitment. Target completion: December 2024.

4. OVERALL ASSURANCE RATING

The overall assurance rating for financial controls in Q3 2024 is MODERATE.
The dual-authorisation finding (Section 2.1) is assessed as the most
significant risk and requires prompt remediation.
