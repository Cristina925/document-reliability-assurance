SECTION 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1  This Data Processing Impact Assessment (DPIA) has been prepared in
     accordance with Article 35 of the General Data Protection Regulation
     (GDPR) and the guidance issued by the Information Commissioner's Office.

1.2  The assessment applies to the proposed deployment of a client onboarding
     portal that collects and processes personal data including identification
     documents, financial history, and contact information.

1.3  The assessment covers all processing activities from initial data
     collection through to retention and deletion under the organisation's
     data-retention schedule.

SECTION 2 — DESCRIPTION OF PROCESSING

2.1  Data categories collected:
     (a) Full legal name and date of birth
     (b) Residential address history (24 months)
     (c) Government-issued identification number
     (d) Employment status and income range
     (e) Bank account details for payment processing
     (f) Device identifiers and IP address (for fraud prevention)

2.2  Legal basis for processing:
     Processing is carried out on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR
     (performance of a contract) for categories (a)–(e) and Article 6(1)(f)
     GDPR (legitimate interests) for category (f).

2.3  Data retention: All personal data will be retained for seven years
     following the conclusion of the contractual relationship, in accordance
     with financial services regulatory requirements, after which it will be
     securely deleted.

SECTION 3 — NECESSITY AND PROPORTIONALITY

3.1  The processing is limited to information strictly necessary for the
     purposes described. Alternative approaches that would achieve the same
     purpose with less personal data have been evaluated and are not
     operationally feasible at the scale required.

3.2  The collection of identification documents is required by Anti-Money
     Laundering Regulations 2017 (SI 2017/692) and cannot be reduced
     without creating a regulatory compliance gap.
