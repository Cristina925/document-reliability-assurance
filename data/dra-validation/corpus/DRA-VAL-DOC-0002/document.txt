EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This Clinical Governance Annual Report covers the period 1 January to
31 December 2024. It is submitted to the Trust Board in fulfilment of the
requirements of the Health and Social Care Act 2012 and NHS England's Clinical
Governance Framework.

During the reporting period, the Trust achieved a 94% compliance rate with the
Duty of Candour, against a national benchmark of 91%. Patient safety incident
reporting increased by 12% year-on-year, consistent with the Trust's strategy
of improving the culture of safety reporting.

SECTION 1 — PATIENT SAFETY

1.1  Serious Incidents
     In the reporting period, 7 serious incidents were declared to NHS England.
     Of these, 5 have been fully investigated and closed; 2 remain under
     active review. No Never Events were recorded during the period.

1.2  Learning from Incidents
     The Trust's Patient Safety Learning Group met quarterly. A total of
     43 learning actions were issued following root cause analyses, of which
     39 have been closed and implemented. Four actions relating to medicines
     management remain in progress and are due for completion by Q1 2025.

1.3  Falls Prevention
     The inpatient falls rate reduced from 6.2 per 1,000 bed days in 2023 to
     5.8 per 1,000 bed days in 2024, representing a statistically significant
     improvement (p < 0.05).

SECTION 2 — CLINICAL EFFECTIVENESS

2.1  Mortality Review
     All deaths occurring within 30 days of an inpatient episode were subject
     to structured judgement review. In 2024, 214 reviews were completed. Of
     these, 6 (2.8%) were assessed as having a degree of avoidability.
     Learning from these reviews has been shared with relevant clinical teams.

2.2  Audit Programme
     The Trust completed 87% of its planned clinical audit programme in 2024,
     against a target of 90%. Three audits were deferred to 2025 due to
     staffing pressures in the relevant clinical areas.

2.3  Compliance with NICE Guidance
     The Trust achieved full compliance with 23 of 27 applicable NICE quality
     standards. Outstanding compliance gaps relate to dementia care (QS30),
     chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (QS10), and long-term conditions
     care planning (QS17). Action plans have been agreed for each.

SECTION 3 — PATIENT EXPERIENCE

3.1  The Trust's Friends and Family Test score for inpatient services was 93%
     (recommended or highly recommended) in 2024, above the national average
     of 89%.

3.2  A total of 312 formal complaints were received. 89% were resolved within
     the 40-working-day target. The most frequent complaint category was
     communication (28%), followed by treatment outcomes (21%).

3.3  The Patient Experience Team conducted 6 listening events during the year,
     with direct learning fed into the Trust's Quality Improvement Programme.

SECTION 4 — WORKFORCE AND STAFFING

4.1  The Trust's average registered nursing vacancy rate was 8.2% in 2024,
     down from 11.4% in 2023. Mandatory training compliance stood at 92%
     against a target of 95%.

4.2  A clinical supervision framework was introduced for all band 5 and above
     nursing staff. Implementation will be assessed in the Q1 2025 audit.
