# DRA-PUB-002 — Phase 1: Publication Evidence Inventory and Claim-Boundary Audit

**Status:** Phase 1 complete. **Scope:** audit and publication-governance only — no evaluator, corpus, GC-1, GEN-001, VAL-002, historical result, or proof-receipt file was modified.

## 1. Executive Verdict

**READY_WITH_REMEDIATIONS**

The DRA programme's evidence base is intact, internally consistent, and re-verified (all identity checks below pass with no drift). Nothing discovered in this audit is a material defect in evidence identity, reproducibility, or the defensibility of the core claims. However, the programme has never previously been audited from a *public-release* standpoint, and this audit surfaces concrete remediations that must be completed **before** any repository content or manuscript is made public — chiefly: (a) raw third-party document bytes are stored in-repo and must be licence-cleared file-by-file before release, not just corpus-admission-cleared; (b) several draft claim phrasings in circulation (e.g. informal descriptions of GEN-001/VAL-002 as "independently validated") are `PROHIBITED_OVERCLAIM` and must be corrected to the terminology fixed in §6; (c) no external/third-party validation exists yet and this must be stated affirmatively, not left implicit. None of these require touching evidence-bearing artefacts — all are documentation/packaging remediations, which is why the verdict is `READY_WITH_REMEDIATIONS` rather than `PUBLICATION_BLOCKED`.

## 6. GEN-001 / VAL-002 Independence-Terminology Determination

Precise meaning established by direct textual audit (see exploration transcript basis):

- **"Blind" in both studies** means *prospective, pre-registered, contamination-blind*: no document was inspected by a human or by DRA tooling for the purpose of predicting DRA's likely performance before being locked into the sample manifest. It does **not** mean a blinded human evaluator, and it does **not** mean the evaluator itself was denied access to document content — DRA necessarily reads the full document to evaluate it.
- **"Independent" in both studies**, wherever it appears, means one of: independent evidence/sample (relative to GEN-001's own denominators, in VAL-002's case), independent recomputation/re-verification (digest re-derivation), independent observations (statistical/deduplication units), or independence from commercial considerations. It does **not** mean independent-of-DRA-developers organisational review, except in one place — `DRA-GEN-001-POST-BLIND-EVIDENCE-REVIEW.md:385-386` — which explicitly and correctly states that *this* meaning did **not** apply: "Independent external validation (this review, like Phase 2, was performed internally, not by an external party)."
- **"External" is used only for acquisition/network conditions** (`EXTERNAL_ACQUISITION_FAILURE`, "externally blocked," "external, publisher-side behaviour") — never to denote a third-party reviewer.
- **No third party — no external organisation, no separate research group, no external human reviewer — participated in the design, execution, or review of GEN-001 or VAL-002.** The unrelated `DRA-VAL-001A..F` programme did use external human reviewers, but VAL-002 explicitly disclaims any relationship to it.

**Publication-safe terminology (mandatory going forward):**

| Do say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| "a pre-registered, prospective, contamination-blind internal study" | "an independently validated study" |
| "evidence independent of the development corpus and of each other" (GEN-001 vs VAL-002) | "externally validated evidence" |
| "designed and executed by the DRA research programme itself, under a frozen protocol, against previously unseen documents" | "reviewed by an independent third party" |
| "no external/third-party validation has yet been performed" (stated affirmatively, in the limitations section) | silence on this point, or implying it via loose use of "independent" |

## 7. Public-Release / Licensing Classification

Distinguishing categories per the task's request:

1. **DRA-authored material** (spec docs, evaluator source, test code, all `docs/dra/*.md` analysis/review reports, all `analysis/*.ts` synthesis modules): **PUBLIC_SAFE.** This is the actual object of publication.
2. **Third-party source documents referenced by the corpus/GEN-001/VAL-002**: the corpus's own schema (`corpus/schema.ts`) and GEN-001's frame/eligibility JSON files store **metadata + digest only** (URL, publisher, licence basis, SHA-256, word count) — confirmed by direct inspection, not full text. These metadata records are **PUBLIC_SAFE**.
3. **Permitted excerpts**: short in-context quotations already embedded in `docs/dra` reports (e.g. the "es preciso" adversarial-probe sentences, footnote/citation excerpts used to demonstrate a defect) are brief, transformative (used to document DRA's own behaviour), and drawn from licences that permit reuse (OGL, public domain, CC BY). **PUBLIC_SAFE** as currently used; do not extend to longer verbatim reproduction.
4. **Hashes/digests and metadata**: all digest fields (`GC1_AGGREGATE_DIGEST`, per-document `integrityDigest`, `contentDigest`, `substantiveDigest`, etc.) are **PUBLIC_SAFE** — one-way hashes carry no redistribution risk.
5. **Licence-governed full-text artefacts actually stored in the repository**: `lib/dra-reference/src/benchmark/analysis/val-002-phase1/data/raw/*.bin` contain the complete raw HTML bytes of third-party government documents (ONS, GOV.UK, US-federal FTC/EPA/Census sources). Verified licence bases present: **Open Government Licence v3.0** (permits copying, publishing, and adapting with attribution) and **US federal public domain, 17 U.S.C. §105** (no copyright restriction). Classification: **REQUIRES_REVIEW** — not because either licence class is restrictive, but because this audit is the *first* time these files have been evaluated for a *public-repository-release* purpose (as opposed to internal corpus-admission/reproducibility purposes); a short, file-by-file confirmation pass (checking each `.bin`'s recorded `licenceBasis` field against its actual source page's current terms) should be done immediately before release, since licence terms and page content can change over time between admission and publication.
6. **Governance/acquisition module capability to store full text (`ContentPayload.content`, `CorpusCandidate.sourceContent`/`generatedContent`)**: this capability exists in code for admission-time duplicate detection but is **not persisted to any committed file** — confirmed by repository search. Classification: **INTERNAL_DEVELOPMENT_ONLY** by current construction; flagged only so a future engineering change doesn't silently start persisting full third-party text without going through this same licence check.
7. **Material that should be referenced by canonical source URL rather than redistributed**: any future non-OGL/non-public-domain/non-CC-BY document admitted to the corpus (none currently observed to be stored as raw bytes) would fall here — e.g. the previously-noted "NAO copyright (not OGL)" and Bulgarian "CC BY-ND" admissions are governance-record entries; **this audit did not find raw byte storage for those specific documents** (only VAL-002's `data/raw/` directory was found to contain persisted raw bytes, and its two licence bases are both permissive). If any additional raw-byte caches exist elsewhere in the repository under different naming, Phase 2 should re-run the same `find`-and-classify sweep before packaging.
8. **Internal/development-only material**: scratch test directories (e.g. `execution/__tests__/.bmk-023-scratch/*.json`, several megabytes of intermediate benchmark text cache) are build/test artefacts, not intended for a public package regardless of licence status — recommend excluding the entire `__tests__/.bmk-*-scratch/` pattern from any release bundle.

**Important general finding**: admission into the research corpus (governance approval) is a *content-integrity and non-contamination* gate, not a *redistribution-rights* gate — the codebase has no field anywhere (`redistributable`, `publicDistribution`, `fullTextAllowed`, etc.) that records a redistribution decision. This audit had to reconstruct redistribution safety manually from `licenceBasis` metadata; Phase 2 should consider (as a documentation-only recommendation, not an engineering task under this audit's mandate) explicitly recording a "public-release-cleared" flag per stored-raw-bytes artefact rather than relying on the corpus-admission licence field for a second, different purpose.
