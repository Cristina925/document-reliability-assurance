# DRA-VAL-001C — Reviewer Qualification Procedure

**Status: OPERATIVE** | Effective from 2026-07-27

---

## 1. Qualification Sequence

All applicants must complete the following 13 steps in order. No step may be skipped.

| Step | Action | Gate |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Application received | Application reference issued (APP-NNNN) |
| 2 | Identity and contact details verified | Real person confirmed, contact details valid |
| 3 | Experience evidence reviewed | Evidence items assessed; domain coverage classified |
| 4 | Domain coverage classified | Domains confirmed with evidence basis |
| 5 | Conflict declaration submitted | Complete COI form received and timestamped |
| 6 | Conflict independently assessed | Assessment completed by independent assessor |
| 7 | Confidentiality and consent accepted | Both agreements explicitly confirmed |
| 8 | Protocol orientation completed | Reviewer confirms understanding of the review protocol |
| 9 | General qualification exercise completed | Submission received and timestamped |
| 10 | Domain exercise completed (where required) | Submission received for each claimed domain |
| 11 | Qualification independently scored | Scores and qualitative assessment completed |
| 12 | Eligibility decision recorded | Outcome determined and documented |
| 13 | Reviewer status registered | Registry entry created or updated |

### Step Dependencies

Steps 5–7 must be completed before Steps 8–11. A reviewer with a disqualifying conflict assessed at Step 6 proceeds no further. A reviewer who has not accepted consent and confidentiality (Step 7) may not access qualification exercise materials.

---

## 2. Qualification Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **QUALIFIED_GENERAL** | Qualified as a general assurance reviewer. May review any corpus document within workload limits. |
| **QUALIFIED_DOMAIN_SPECIALIST** | Qualified as a domain specialist in one or more domains. Preferred assignment to specialist domains. |
| **QUALIFIED_ADJUDICATOR** | Qualified as an adjudicator. Requires prior QUALIFIED_GENERAL or QUALIFIED_DOMAIN_SPECIALIST status. May act as adjudicator subject to per-document restrictions. |
| **CONDITIONALLY_QUALIFIED** | Qualified with explicit restrictions (e.g. specific domain limitations, paired-reviewer requirement, adjudicator exclusion). |
| **REQUIRES_MORE_EVIDENCE** | Qualification cannot be assessed; additional experience evidence or exercise performance needed. |
| **NOT_QUALIFIED** | Qualification assessment concluded; applicant does not meet the required standard. |
| **DISQUALIFIED_CONFLICT** | Disqualified due to an assessed disqualifying conflict of interest. |
| **WITHDRAWN** | Applicant withdrew before or during the qualification process. |

---

## 3. Minimum Qualification Dimensions

All qualification assessments must address all 10 dimensions:

| Dimension | Pass Threshold | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| ISSUE_IDENTIFICATION | 60/100 | Correct identification of issues present in exercise document |
| MATERIALITY_ASSESSMENT | 60/100 | Accurate assessment of which issues are material |
| EVIDENCE_SOURCE_COMPARISON | 60/100 | Correct comparison of document claims against source evidence |
| AUTHORITY_AND_SCOPE_ASSESSMENT | 60/100 | Correct identification of authority and scope problems |
| ISSUE_SEPARATION | 60/100 | Distinct issues recorded separately |
| SEVERITY_ASSESSMENT | 60/100 | Severity assignments consistent with definitions |
| RELEASE_RECOMMENDATION | 60/100 | Release recommendation consistent with findings |
| UNCERTAINTY_RECORDING | 60/100 | Uncertainty recorded appropriately without overclaiming |
| PROTOCOL_COMPLIANCE | 65/100 | Adherence to the frozen review protocol (higher threshold) |
| WRITTEN_RATIONALE_QUALITY | 60/100 | Findings documented clearly with specific evidence reference |

A pass on all 10 dimensions is required for QUALIFIED_GENERAL or better. Failure on any critical dimension requires a REQUIRES_MORE_EVIDENCE or NOT_QUALIFIED outcome.

---

## 4. Scoring Basis

- Scoring is structured across dimensions, not based on word-for-word agreement with expected answers.
- **Credit for defensible alternative findings:** A reviewer who identifies a genuine issue not captured in the reference answer receives positive credit. Penalising reviewers for finding valid but unanticipated issues is prohibited.
- **Qualitative assessment required:** The assessor must provide a narrative assessment for every candidate, including explanation of how defensible alternatives were treated.
- **Pass threshold is minimum:** Assessors may recommend conditional qualification where overall performance is satisfactory but specific areas need strengthening.

---

## 5. Identity Verification Requirement

Qualification requires verification that the applicant is a genuine person with genuine qualifications. The following are prohibited:

- Accepting applications from unverifiable persons
- Qualifying anonymous applicants who cannot be identified to the programme
- Treating AI-generated or synthetic application data as a genuine application
- Counting unverified applicants toward the reviewer pool

---

## 6. Adjudicator Path

For QUALIFIED_ADJUDICATOR status, the applicant must:
1. First hold QUALIFIED_GENERAL or QUALIFIED_DOMAIN_SPECIALIST status.
2. Complete and pass the adjudicator qualification exercise.
3. The qualification decision must explicitly confirm prior reviewer qualification before adjudicator status is granted.

A reviewer who is only CONDITIONALLY_QUALIFIED may not proceed to adjudicator qualification without the conditions being resolved.

---

## 7. Re-qualification

- Reviewers qualified for one execution window do not require full re-qualification for subsequent windows if:
  - Less than 12 months have elapsed since qualification.
  - No new disqualifying conflict has arisen.
  - They confirm consent and availability for the new window.
- After 12 months, a new exercise must be completed.
- After a conflict reassessment triggers, full qualification process must restart.

---

## 8. Qualification Decision Governance

- All qualification decisions must be made by an assessor who is not the applicant.
- Assessors must not have a personal or financial interest in the applicant's outcome.
- Decisions are recorded with the assessor's identifier, timestamp, and rationale.
- The programme lead has final approval authority over all qualification decisions.

---

## 9. Record Keeping

All qualification records are retained for 10 years after programme completion. Records include:
- Application form
- Experience evidence references
- COI declaration and assessment
- Consent and confidentiality records
- Exercise submissions
- Scoring records with assessor notes
- Eligibility decision with rationale
