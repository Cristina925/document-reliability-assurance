# DRA-VAL-001C — Reviewer Eligibility Standard

**Status: OPERATIVE** | Effective from DRA-VAL-001C milestone activation (2026-07-27)

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines binding eligibility requirements for each reviewer category. Eligibility is assessed by an independent assessor — not by the applicant. No formal credential is required where equivalent demonstrable experience is sufficient.

---

## 2A. General Assurance Reviewer Eligibility

### Minimum Requirements

All of the following must be satisfied:

| Requirement | Minimum Standard |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Professional experience** | Demonstrated professional experience reviewing consequential documents, assurance outputs, compliance materials, risk reports, governance documents, technical reports, policies, or equivalent |
| **Critical analysis capability** | Demonstrated ability to identify unsupported claims, evidence gaps, authority gaps, scope problems, internal contradictions, and release-significant defects |
| **Documentation capability** | Demonstrated ability to document findings clearly, specifically, and reproducibly |
| **Protocol compliance** | Willingness and ability to follow a frozen written review protocol without deviation |
| **Conflict of interest** | No disqualifying conflict of interest |
| **Qualification exercises** | Successful completion of the general qualification exercise |
| **Consent and confidentiality** | Acceptance of the programme consent agreement and confidentiality obligations |

### Permitted Evidence

The following types of evidence are accepted:
- CV or professional profile showing relevant roles and responsibilities
- Professional references from peers, managers, or clients
- Published work (reports, assessments, audits, reviews) demonstrating relevant competence
- Regulatory registration or certification relevant to the claim
- Academic credentials in a relevant discipline
- Portfolio samples (redacted as required to protect confidentiality)

### Insufficient Evidence

The following are not sufficient on their own:
- Academic credentials alone without demonstrated professional review experience
- Job titles without role description or evidence of review activities
- Self-declarations without supporting evidence
- Unverifiable claims

### Disqualification

A general assurance reviewer is disqualified when any of the following apply:
- A disqualifying conflict of interest is assessed
- Qualification exercise score fails on any critical dimension
- The applicant submits materially false information
- The applicant is found to have accessed evaluator outputs

### Suspension

Suspension (pending investigation) applies when:
- A potential conflict comes to light after qualification
- A protocol violation is reported or suspected
- A submission-integrity question arises

### Requalification

A reviewer who withdrew voluntarily may requalify for a subsequent execution window by completing updated consent and conflict processes. No requirement to repeat exercises unless more than 12 months have elapsed.

### Appeals and Reassessment

An applicant who is assessed NOT_QUALIFIED may request a reassessment specifying the grounds. The reassessment must be conducted by a different assessor. The reassessment outcome is final.

## 2B. Domain Specialist Eligibility

Domain specialists must meet all general assurance reviewer requirements and additionally:

| Requirement | Minimum Standard |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Domain expertise** | Relevant professional, academic, regulatory, legal, clinical, technical, financial, governance, procurement, HR, policy, or operational expertise in at least one corpus domain |
| **Experience evidence** | Evidence of domain-specific experience: minimum 2 years relevant professional practice, or equivalent academic or specialist competence |
| **Domain exercise** | Successful completion of the domain-specific qualification exercise for the claimed domain(s) |

### Domain-Specific Expertise Standards

| Domain | Accepted Expertise |
|--------|-------------------|
| LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY | Legal practitioners, regulatory compliance professionals, policy analysts, compliance managers, regulatory advisers |
| HEALTHCARE_AND_LIFE_SCIENCES | Clinical practitioners, healthcare administrators, clinical governance professionals, healthcare regulators, NHS/health system managers |
| FINANCE_AND_ACCOUNTING | Finance professionals, accountants, auditors, CFO/treasury function professionals, financial regulators |
| CYBERSECURITY_AND_TECHNICAL_ASSURANCE | Information security professionals, CISO/security management, security auditors, technical assurance professionals, penetration testers with governance exposure |
| BUSINESS_AND_EXECUTIVE_REPORTING | Company secretaries, governance professionals, non-executive directors, internal audit leaders, risk management professionals |
| PROCUREMENT_AND_THIRD_PARTY_RISK | Procurement professionals, vendor risk managers, supply chain compliance professionals, third-party assurance practitioners |
| HR_AND_WORKPLACE_POLICY | HR professionals, employment lawyers, people operations professionals, workplace policy specialists |
| PUBLIC_POLICY_AND_GOVERNANCE | Policy analysts, civil servants, public administration professionals, regulatory analysts, political scientists with applied practice |
| GENERAL_OPERATIONAL | Operations managers, business analysts, process professionals, quality managers with documented review experience |

### Equivalent Experience

A formal qualification is not required where equivalent demonstrable professional experience exists. The assessor determines equivalence based on the evidence provided.

### Conditional Eligibility

An applicant may be conditionally eligible for a domain if they meet most but not all requirements. Conditional eligibility carries explicit restrictions (e.g. paired with a specialist reviewer; may not act as sole specialist for a domain).

## 2C. Adjudicator Eligibility

Adjudicators must meet all general assurance reviewer requirements and additionally:

| Requirement | Minimum Standard |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Prior qualification** | Must first be qualified as QUALIFIED_GENERAL or QUALIFIED_DOMAIN_SPECIALIST |
| **Seniority / experience** | Demonstrated seniority or evidence-evaluation experience beyond general reviewer competence (e.g. senior audit partner, chief examiner, academic peer reviewer, senior regulatory inspector) |
| **Adjudication capability** | Demonstrated ability to resolve disagreements between competent professionals based on reasoning, evidence, and protocol compliance |
| **Rule acceptance** | Explicit acceptance of the frozen comparison and adjudication rules (DRA-VAL-001C-COMPARISON-PROTOCOL.md) |
| **Adjudicator exercise** | Successful completion of the adjudicator qualification exercise |
| **Independence from document** | Must not adjudicate any document they reviewed as a primary reviewer |

### Conflict with Primary Reviewer Role

An adjudicator who is also a primary reviewer on a given document is automatically excluded from adjudicating that document. Assignment systems must enforce this.

## 3. Qualification Dimensions

All reviewers are assessed across the following dimensions in qualification exercises:

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| ISSUE_IDENTIFICATION | Ability to identify issues in the document |
| MATERIALITY_ASSESSMENT | Ability to assess whether identified issues are material |
| EVIDENCE_SOURCE_COMPARISON | Ability to compare document claims against source material |
| AUTHORITY_AND_SCOPE_ASSESSMENT | Ability to assess the authority and scope of claims |
| ISSUE_SEPARATION | Ability to separate distinct issues without conflation |
| SEVERITY_ASSESSMENT | Ability to assign severity levels consistently and appropriately |
| RELEASE_RECOMMENDATION | Ability to form a release recommendation |
| UNCERTAINTY_RECORDING | Ability to record uncertainty without overstating confidence |
| PROTOCOL_COMPLIANCE | Adherence to the frozen review protocol |
| WRITTEN_RATIONALE_QUALITY | Quality and specificity of written rationale for each finding |

---

## 4. Scoring Basis

- Scoring is structured across all 10 dimensions with a minimum pass score of 60/100 per dimension.
- Reviewers are NOT scored solely on agreement with expected answers.
- Credit is given for defensible alternative findings — issues the reviewer identifies that are genuine but not anticipated in the reference answers.
- A qualitative narrative assessment is mandatory alongside all scores.
- A reviewer who identifies all reference issues plus defensible alternatives scores above a reviewer who identifies only reference issues.

---

## 5. Expired or Stale Evidence

Experience evidence older than 10 years without subsequent relevant activity may be treated as stale. The assessor determines whether stale evidence is still probative given the overall experience record.

---

## 6. Scientific-Independence Requirements

Scientific-independence requirements may not be lowered merely to fill reviewer slots. If the minimum reviewer pool cannot be genuinely assembled, the programme records the gap and DRA-VAL-001D remains blocked.
