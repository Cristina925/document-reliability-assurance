**Document reference:** DRA-VAL-001C-RECRUITMENT-PRIORITIES  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Status:** Active — Wave 1 outreach not yet started

## 1. Overview

This document specifies the priority order for reviewer recruitment across two recruitment waves. Wave 1 targets the minimum viable pilot reviewer pool required to achieve NOT_READY → READY on the pilot readiness assessment. Wave 2 extends coverage to the full benchmark corpus.

All constraints — independence, conflict, workload, and adjudication — apply to every reviewer regardless of wave. A reviewer who appears in multiple domain categories counts once toward headcount but may provide coverage across multiple domains.

---

## 2. Pilot Coverage Requirements

The pilot corpus (`DRA-VAL-PILOT-001-PARTIAL`) contains 7 documents across 7 domains. The readiness assessment requires:

| Requirement | Minimum | Preferred |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Genuine qualified reviewers | 6 | 10–12 |
| Independent reviewers per document | 2 | 3 |
| Qualified adjudicators | 1 | 2 |
| Domains with ≥ 2 reviewers | 7 (all pilot domains) | 7 |

**7 pilot domains (each requiring ≥ 2 eligible, independent reviewers):**
- Legal and Regulatory
- Healthcare and Life Sciences
- Finance and Accounting
- Cybersecurity and Technical Assurance
- Business and Executive Reporting
- Procurement and Third-Party Risk
- HR and Workplace Policy

*(Public Policy and Governance and General Operational are represented in the full 20-document pilot design but not in the current 7-document partial freeze.)*

## 4. Wave 2 — Extended Benchmark Coverage

Wave 2 recruiting extends coverage to the remaining pilot domains (outside the current 7-document partial freeze) and builds toward the full 120-document benchmark corpus. Wave 2 may proceed in parallel with Wave 1 where prospects are identified but should not delay Wave 1 qualification.

### Wave 2 Target Domains

| Domain | Pilot Documents (Planned) | Wave 2 Minimum Reviewers | Notes |
|--------|--------------------------|--------------------------|-------|
| Finance and Accounting | 3 | +1 domain specialist | Wave 1 general coverage is sufficient for 1 pilot doc; a specialist is needed for quality across 3 docs |
| Procurement and Third-Party Risk | 2 | +1 domain specialist | Complex supply chain and third-party risk documents benefit from specialist expertise |
| Business and Executive Reporting | 2 | +1 domain specialist | Executive and board-level reporting requires familiarity with corporate governance standards |
| HR and Workplace Policy | 2 | +1 domain specialist | Employment law, workplace investigation, and policy documents require HR-specific knowledge |
| Public Policy and Governance | 2 | +1 domain specialist | Policy and governance documents require public-sector or regulatory experience |
| General Operational | 2 | No specialist required | Operational procedures are well covered by general assurance reviewers |

### Wave 2 Additional Adjudicator

A second adjudicator is strongly preferred for the full benchmark. A single adjudicator covering 120 documents across 9 domains represents a significant workload and creates a single point of failure. Wave 2 should target at least one additional qualified adjudicator.

### Wave 2 Preferred Headcount Targets

| Role | Wave 1 | Wave 2 Addition | Total Preferred |
|------|--------|----------------|----------------|
| General Assurance Reviewers | 2 | +2–4 | 4–6 |
| Domain Specialists | 3 | +5 | 8 |
| Adjudicators | 1 | +1 | 2 |
| **Total** | **6** | **+8–10** | **14–16** |

These are preferred targets. Eligibility verification, conflict screening, and independent qualification remain mandatory for every reviewer regardless of how urgently coverage is needed.

## 5. Expertise Overlap and Headcount Reduction

A single reviewer may qualify as both a general assurance reviewer and a domain specialist (or in multiple domains). Expertise overlap may reduce the raw headcount needed. However, the following constraints cannot be relaxed regardless of overlap:

| Constraint | Rule | Cannot Be Waived |
|-----------|------|-----------------|
| Two independent reviewers per document | Each document requires two reviewers with no coordination before submission | Yes |
| Conflict independence | Each reviewer must pass independent conflict assessment | Yes |
| Adjudicator independence | Adjudicator must not be an original reviewer of any document they adjudicate | Yes |
| Workload limits | No reviewer is assigned beyond their declared capacity | Yes |
| Self-qualification | No reviewer may approve their own eligibility decision | Yes |

**Example:** A reviewer with both legal and healthcare expertise may cover two pilot documents. This reduces the headcount from 8 (2 per domain × 4 domains) to potentially 6 (2 general + 4 specialists with coverage overlap). But two independent reviewers per document, and one non-conflicted adjudicator, remain mandatory.

## 6. Priority Ordering Rationale

Wave 1 priorities are ordered by:

1. **Blocking criticality** — the adjudicator criterion (RC-08) is one of the 8 critical criteria that prevent READY status; it cannot be satisfied by general reviewers
2. **Domain specificity** — domain specialist roles in legal, healthcare, and cybersecurity are prioritised because those domains appear in the pilot corpus and general reviewers may lack sufficient depth
3. **Pipeline efficiency** — general reviewer recruitment runs in parallel with specialist recruitment, since the qualification procedure is the same; early general recruits can begin qualification while specialist recruitment continues
4. **Risk of disqualification** — domain specialists in sensitive areas (legal, finance) carry slightly higher conflict risk; starting with clear-boundary domains reduces pipeline risk

Wave 2 priority ordering follows document count in the full pilot corpus design — domains with more planned pilot documents are prioritised.

## 7. Recruitment Actions and Tracking

All recruitment activity must be recorded in `DRA-VAL-001C-RECRUITMENT-OUTREACH-TRACKER.md` before outreach is conducted.

**Per-prospect record required fields:**
- Prospect reference (PROS-NNNN)
- Domain(s)
- Source of introduction
- Contact channel and date
- Response status
- Conflict risk pre-screening note
- Next action

Outreach should use the templates in `DRA-VAL-001C-OUTREACH-MESSAGE-PACK.md`, adapted to the specific role (Template A for general contacts, Template B for domain specialists, Template C for adjudicators).

---

## 8. Readiness Gating

The transition from Wave 1 to DRA-VAL-001D requires:

1. All 6 Wave 1 roles filled with genuine, qualified reviewers
2. Pilot reviewer readiness assessment formally updated to READY
3. Corpus manifest integrity re-verified immediately before the review window opens
4. Assignment plan prepared and frozen (randomisation seed generated)
5. No reviewer has accessed expected document findings

Wave 2 does not gate DRA-VAL-001D. Wave 2 completes in parallel with DRA-VAL-001D and gates the full benchmark execution (DRA-VAL-001E).
