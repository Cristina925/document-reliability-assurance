# DRA-VAL-001C — Qualification Guide

**For applicants to the DRA Scientific Validation Reviewer Programme**

---

## Purpose

This guide explains the qualification process, what you will be asked to do, and how your performance will be assessed. Read it before beginning any exercise.

---

## What Qualification Is For

Qualification establishes that you are capable of performing independent document review at the standard required by the DRA Scientific Validation Protocol. It is not designed to test your knowledge of DRA, CTS, or evaluator rules. You are being qualified as an independent professional reviewer, not as an expert in this software system.

---

## What the Exercises Test

The exercises assess 10 dimensions of review capability:

| Dimension | What It Assesses |
|-----------|-----------------|
| ISSUE_IDENTIFICATION | Can you find issues that are genuinely present? |
| MATERIALITY_ASSESSMENT | Can you assess whether issues are material? |
| EVIDENCE_SOURCE_COMPARISON | Can you compare claims against source evidence? |
| AUTHORITY_AND_SCOPE_ASSESSMENT | Can you identify authority and scope problems? |
| ISSUE_SEPARATION | Do you record each distinct issue separately? |
| SEVERITY_ASSESSMENT | Do you assign severity levels consistently? |
| RELEASE_RECOMMENDATION | Is your release recommendation consistent with your findings? |
| UNCERTAINTY_RECORDING | Do you record uncertainty honestly without overclaiming? |
| PROTOCOL_COMPLIANCE | Do you follow the review protocol as written? |
| WRITTEN_RATIONALE_QUALITY | Do you explain your findings specifically and clearly? |

---

## What the Exercises Do NOT Test

- Your knowledge of DRA or CTS
- Your agreement with any software system's outputs
- Your ability to guess what the programme expects to find
- Loyalty to any particular outcome
- Your familiarity with prior validation work

---

## Exercise Documents

Qualification exercises use purpose-built training documents. These are NOT:
- Documents from the scientific benchmark corpus
- Documents used in DRA development or testing
- Documents with known evaluator outputs

Each training document is clearly labelled as qualification/training material.

---

## Scoring Basis

- Each dimension is scored 0–100.
- Pass threshold: 60/100 per dimension (65/100 for PROTOCOL_COMPLIANCE).
- **Credit is given for defensible alternative findings** — issues you identify that are genuine but not anticipated in the reference answers. Identifying real issues beyond the reference is rewarded, not penalised.
- A qualitative narrative assessment is completed alongside all scores.
- You are NOT penalised for identifying issues that the reference answers do not include, as long as your reasoning is sound.

---

## How to Approach the Exercises

1. Read the document carefully before recording anything.
2. Identify every issue you believe is genuinely present.
3. Record each distinct issue separately.
4. Assess materiality and severity for each issue.
5. Compare against source evidence where provided.
6. Form a release recommendation consistent with your findings.
7. Record your confidence level honestly.
8. Submit your findings independently — do not consult colleagues.

---

## Exercise Completion

- All exercises must be completed within the time window specified in your invitation.
- Exercises must be completed independently — do not seek guidance that might influence your findings.
- Once submitted, exercise responses cannot be changed.

---

## Exercise Types

| Exercise | Required For |
|----------|-------------|
| GENERAL_ASSURANCE (QEX-0001) | All applicants |
| DOMAIN_SPECIALIST (QEX-0002 to QEX-0009) | Applicants claiming domain specialist status |
| ADJUDICATOR (QEX-0010) | Applicants for adjudicator status (after general qualification) |

---

## Results

You will receive:
- Pass/Fail per dimension
- Overall qualification outcome
- Brief narrative feedback

You will NOT receive:
- The reference answer list
- A comparison with any evaluator output
- A comparison with other applicants' submissions

---

## If You Are Not Qualified

If your assessment results in NOT_QUALIFIED or REQUIRES_MORE_EVIDENCE, you will receive feedback and may request a reassessment. A different assessor will conduct the reassessment.
