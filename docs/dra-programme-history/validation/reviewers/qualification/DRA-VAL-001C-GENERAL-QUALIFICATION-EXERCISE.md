### Meridian Holdings plc  
### Annual Risk Management Statement  
### Financial Year 2023

---

**Introduction**

The Board of Meridian Holdings plc is pleased to present its annual risk management statement for the financial year ended 31 December 2023. This statement describes the principal risks facing the Group, the controls and mitigations in place, and our assessment of the Group's overall risk position.

The Board is satisfied that the Group's risk management framework is operating effectively and that all principal risks are adequately mitigated.

---

**Principal Risks**

**Risk 1: Regulatory Compliance Risk**

The Group operates across four regulatory jurisdictions. Compliance with applicable laws and regulations is monitored by the Compliance Committee, which meets quarterly. No significant regulatory breaches were identified during the year.

*Mitigation:* Regular compliance monitoring. External legal counsel retained for material regulatory questions.

*Residual risk:* LOW

---

**Risk 2: Cybersecurity Risk**

The Group's information systems are protected by industry-standard security controls. The Group has not experienced any material security incidents during the financial year.

*Mitigation:* Annual penetration testing. Staff awareness training (last conducted: March 2022). Endpoint protection deployed across all devices.

*Residual risk:* LOW

---

**Risk 3: Key Person Dependency**

The Group is dependent on the continued service of its Chief Executive Officer, Dr. A. Whitmore, and Chief Financial Officer, Ms. B. Nkosi. The Board considers this risk to be appropriately managed.

*Mitigation:* Succession planning reviewed annually by the Nominations Committee.

*Residual risk:* MEDIUM

---

**Risk 4: Market Risk**

The Group operates in competitive markets subject to pricing pressure. Revenue was £87.4m in 2023, representing a decline of 12% from the prior year.

*Mitigation:* Ongoing product diversification programme. Three new product lines launched in Q4 2023.

*Residual risk:* MEDIUM

---

**Risk 5: Liquidity Risk**

The Group maintains cash reserves sufficient to meet its obligations for a period of at least 18 months. The Group's banking facilities were renewed in November 2023.

*Mitigation:* Cash flow forecasting model updated monthly. Revolving credit facility of £25m available.

*Residual risk:* LOW

---

**Risk 6: Operational Risk**

The Group's operations depend on reliable performance of its core technology platform. The platform achieved 99.2% uptime during the year.

*Mitigation:* Redundant infrastructure in two data centres. Business continuity plan last tested in January 2020.

*Residual risk:* LOW

---

**Conclusion**

The Board confirms that the risk management framework is effective and that the Group's principal risks are adequately identified, assessed, and mitigated. The Board recommends publication of this statement without modification.
