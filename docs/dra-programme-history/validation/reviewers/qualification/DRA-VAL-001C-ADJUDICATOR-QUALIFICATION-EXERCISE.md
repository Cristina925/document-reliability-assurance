## Purpose

This exercise assesses whether a qualified reviewer has the capability to act as an independent adjudicator for material disagreements between primary reviewers. It requires higher-order reasoning about conflicting evidence assessments, protocol compliance, and defensible interpretation.

**Prerequisites:** You must have completed QEX-0001 and received a QUALIFIED_GENERAL or QUALIFIED_DOMAIN_SPECIALIST outcome before this exercise is issued.

---

## What Adjudicators Do

When two primary reviewers disagree on a material issue — whether an issue exists, whether it is material, or how it should be classified — the adjudicator receives:
- The document being reviewed
- Both reviewer submissions (anonymised)
- A description of the disagreement
- Any relevant source evidence

The adjudicator determines:
1. Whether the disagreement is material (by protocol definition)
2. Which position, if either, is better supported by the evidence and protocol
3. The adjudicated finding (which may differ from both reviewers)
4. The reasoning for the adjudicated position

The adjudicator does not re-review the whole document — only the areas in dispute.

## Exercise: QEX-0010

**Training document: QEX-0010-DOC**  

**Document title:** Northgate Infrastructure Ltd — Supplier Due Diligence Report: Sigma Systems Ltd  
**Document type:** Third-party due diligence report  
**Document purpose:** Approval of Sigma Systems Ltd as a Tier 1 supplier

> **Note:** All organisations and individuals are fictitious.

---

### Document Extract (relevant sections only)

**Section 4 — Financial Stability**

> Sigma Systems Ltd reported revenue of £12.4m in FY2022 and £14.1m in FY2023, representing 13.7% year-on-year growth. The company has been profitable in each of the past three years. Trade references from two long-standing customers have been obtained and are satisfactory. Sigma Systems' current ratio is 1.8, which Northgate's finance team considers adequate.
>
> **Conclusion:** Financial risk is LOW.

**Section 6 — Cybersecurity and Data Handling**

> Sigma Systems holds an active Cyber Essentials Plus certification (renewed March 2024). Sigma Systems has confirmed it has not experienced any material security incidents in the past three years. The supplier has signed Northgate's standard data processing agreement.
>
> **Conclusion:** Cybersecurity risk is LOW.

**Section 8 — Overall Recommendation**

> Northgate's Supplier Assessment Panel has assessed Sigma Systems Ltd and recommends approval as a Tier 1 supplier. The Panel is satisfied that all material risks have been assessed and adequately mitigated.

---

### Source Evidence Available

**Source 1: Northgate Internal Finance Note**

> The current ratio of 1.8 was reported as at December 2022. The most recent available accounts (to December 2023) show a current ratio of 0.91 and a net debt position of £2.1m. The company obtained emergency bridging finance of £1.5m in Q1 2024.

**Source 2: Companies House filing (Sigma Systems Ltd)**

> Annual accounts for FY2023 filed March 2024: Net loss of £0.89m. Retained earnings negative: (£1.2m). Auditors noted a material uncertainty relating to going concern in the audit opinion.

**Source 3: Cyber Essentials Plus certificate**

> Certificate number CE+/2024/00142. Valid from 14 March 2024 to 13 March 2025. Scope: internal IT infrastructure only. Cloud services and third-party development environment not in scope.

---

### Reviewer Submissions (Anonymised)

**Reviewer Alpha — Section 4 findings:**

> *Issue 1:* The report states the current ratio is 1.8 and financial risk is LOW. However, the current ratio cited is from December 2022. The most recent accounts (FY2023) show a current ratio of 0.91 and net debt of £2.1m. The company required emergency bridging finance in Q1 2024. The auditors have noted a going concern qualification. The report's financial risk assessment is based on materially outdated data and does not reflect the current financial position.
> - Severity: CRITICAL
> - Materiality: MATERIAL

**Reviewer Beta — Section 4 findings:**

> *Issue A:* The report should have noted that the current ratio figure is 18 months old. However, the company has obtained bridging finance and the growth trajectory suggests the financial challenges may be temporary. The risk assessment as LOW may be slightly optimistic but I do not consider this a critical defect. There is no evidence of imminent insolvency.
> - Severity: ADVISORY
> - Materiality: NOT_MATERIAL

**Reviewer Alpha — Section 6 findings:**

> *Issue 2:* The Cyber Essentials Plus certificate scope is limited to internal IT infrastructure only. Cloud services and third-party development environments are excluded. The report states the cybersecurity risk is LOW without mentioning the scope limitation. For a Tier 1 supplier handling customer data, the excluded scope may be significant.
> - Severity: SIGNIFICANT
> - Materiality: MATERIAL

**Reviewer Beta — Section 6 findings:**

> *Issue B:* The CE+ scope limitation is noted on the certificate. The report does not cite it. This is a documentation gap. Whether the excluded scope matters depends on how Sigma Systems handles Northgate data, which is not described in the report.
> - Severity: SIGNIFICANT  
> - Materiality: MATERIAL (agree with Alpha on this one)

## Adjudicator Tasks

**Task 1: Disagreement Assessment**

Assess whether the disagreement on Section 4 (Issue 1 vs Issue A) is a material disagreement by protocol definition. A material disagreement exists when:
- Reviewers reach different conclusions about whether an issue exists; OR
- Reviewers agree an issue exists but classify it with different severity at the CRITICAL/SIGNIFICANT boundary; OR
- Reviewers reach different release recommendations because of the disagreement.

**Task 2: Adjudication Decision**

Provide your adjudicated finding on the Section 4 financial risk issue:
- Is the issue present? Yes / No / Qualified
- Severity: CRITICAL / SIGNIFICANT / ADVISORY
- Materiality: MATERIAL / NOT_MATERIAL
- Your reasoning: [required]
- Which reviewer's position is better supported, and why?

**Task 3: Release Recommendation**

Based on the full picture (both agreed issues and the adjudicated Section 4 issue), what is the adjudicated release recommendation for this document?
