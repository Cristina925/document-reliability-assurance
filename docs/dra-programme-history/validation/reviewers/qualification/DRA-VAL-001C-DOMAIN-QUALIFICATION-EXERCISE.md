> **TRAINING AND QUALIFICATION MATERIAL — NOT SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE**
>
> These domain exercises are purpose-built for reviewer qualification. They are not drawn from the scientific benchmark corpus. All documents, organisations, and individuals are fictitious. Findings produced in these exercises are used solely to assess reviewer domain competence.

---

## Purpose

Domain qualification exercises assess whether a claimed domain specialist has the depth of expertise to identify domain-specific defects that a general reviewer might miss. They supplement the general qualification exercise (QEX-0001) — they do not replace it.

A domain specialist who passes QEX-0001 but fails a domain exercise may be qualified as QUALIFIED_GENERAL but not QUALIFIED_DOMAIN_SPECIALIST for that domain.

## QEX-0002 — Legal and Regulatory Domain Exercise

**Training document: QEX-0002-DOC**

**Document title:** Ashton Retail Group — Data Protection Policy v3.1  
**Document type:** Internal data protection policy  
**Document purpose:** Governing organisational data handling, subject rights, and legal basis for processing

> **Note:** Ashton Retail Group is a fictitious organisation. All personal data references, ICO references, and processing activities are invented for this exercise.

---

### Ashton Retail Group  
### Data Protection Policy  
### Version 3.1 — Effective 1 January 2024

**Policy Owner:** Legal and Compliance  
**Review date:** January 2026

---

**1. Legal Basis for Processing**

Ashton Retail Group processes personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The legal bases relied upon are set out in the Group's Record of Processing Activities. Where consent is relied upon, consent will be obtained before processing commences.

**2. Individual Rights**

Data subjects have the right to access their personal data. Subject access requests will be responded to within 28 days. Data subjects also have the right to erasure, rectification, restriction, and data portability where applicable.

**3. Retention**

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary. Employee records are retained for 6 years after employment ends. Customer records are retained for 3 years after last transaction.

**4. Data Breach Response**

In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Protection Officer will assess the breach and report to the ICO within 72 hours where required. The breach log is maintained by the Legal team.

**5. Third-Party Processors**

Third-party processors are required to sign data processing agreements before any personal data is shared. The Group maintains a processor register.

**6. International Transfers**

The Group does not transfer personal data outside the UK.

**7. DPO Contact**

Queries regarding data protection should be directed to the Group DPO at dpo@ashtonretail.example.com.

---

### Source Evidence for QEX-0002

**Source: Internal Audit Report Extract (Q2 2024)**

> *Finding 3.4:* The SAR response process was audited. Of 12 SARs received in 2023, 4 were not responded to within the statutory 28-day period. One response was sent 47 days after receipt. No extension requests were made by the Group in these cases. No evidence that the Data Protection Officer was notified of the late responses.
>
> *Finding 3.7:* The processor register was last updated in March 2022. Since that date, the Group has contracted with 3 new SaaS providers who receive personal data. No data processing agreements have been signed with these providers. Personal data has been transferred to them under standard terms of service only.
>
> *Finding 3.9:* The Group's marketing platform is operated by a US-headquartered vendor (ClearReach Inc.). Personal data of UK customers (including email addresses and purchase history) is processed on ClearReach's US servers. No transfer mechanism (adequacy decision, SCCs, or IDTA) has been documented.

## QEX-0005 — Cybersecurity Domain Exercise (sample)

**Training document: QEX-0005-DOC**

**Document title:** Bridgewater Financial Services — Information Security Policy Statement  
**Document type:** Information security policy summary statement  
**Document purpose:** Executive-level confirmation of security policy framework status

> **Note:** Bridgewater Financial Services is a fictitious organisation.

---

### Bridgewater Financial Services  
### Information Security Policy Statement  
### Board-approved 15 March 2024

*The Board confirms that Bridgewater Financial Services maintains a comprehensive information security programme in compliance with ISO 27001. All critical systems are protected by multi-factor authentication. Annual penetration testing is conducted by an independent third party. The Group's incident response plan was last exercised in Q1 2024.*

*No material information security incidents have been reported to the Board during the financial year.*

---

### Source Evidence for QEX-0005

**Source: IT Security Team Internal Briefing (February 2024)**

> MFA is deployed on email and the VPN. Core banking application does not support MFA — the application vendor has confirmed that MFA integration is planned for Q4 2025. Customer-facing portal uses SMS OTP only (not FIDO2/authenticator app).
>
> ISO 27001 certification was last renewed in 2021. The recertification audit due in 2024 has been deferred to Q1 2025 due to resource constraints. The Group is currently operating on an expired certificate.
>
> The Q1 2024 incident response exercise was a tabletop exercise covering email phishing only. Ransomware, insider threat, and supply chain scenarios were not exercised. The IRP has not been updated since the exercise.
>
> A significant incident occurred in November 2023: ransomware was deployed on a development server. The server was isolated and rebuilt. The incident was classified as non-material and not reported to the Board. No FCA notification was made. Legal counsel was not consulted on reporting obligations.
