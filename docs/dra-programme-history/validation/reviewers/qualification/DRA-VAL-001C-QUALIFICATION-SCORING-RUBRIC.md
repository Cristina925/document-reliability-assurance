# DRA-VAL-001C — Qualification Scoring Rubric

**Rubric Version:** v1.0  
**Applies to:** QEX-0001, QEX-0002 through QEX-0009, QEX-0010

> **RESTRICTED — ASSESSOR USE ONLY**
>
> This rubric is used by qualification assessors. It must not be shared with applicants before or during exercises. After an applicant has received their result, general feedback may be provided, but the reference answers must not be disclosed.

---

## Scoring Principles

1. **Minimum pass per dimension: 60/100** (65/100 for PROTOCOL_COMPLIANCE)
2. **Defensible alternatives receive credit** — an issue not in the reference answers but genuinely present in the training document must be given credit, not penalised.
3. **Qualitative assessment is mandatory** — every assessment must include a narrative alongside scores.
4. **Agreement with expected answers is not sufficient** — scoring must assess reasoning quality, not just correct outcomes.
5. **A reviewer who identifies all reference issues plus defensible alternatives scores above a reviewer who identifies only reference issues.**

---

## Dimension 1: ISSUE_IDENTIFICATION (0–100)

| Score Range | Criteria |
|-------------|----------|
| 85–100 | Identifies all material issues in the reference answers, plus at least one defensible alternative finding |
| 70–84 | Identifies all material issues in the reference answers |
| 55–69 | Identifies most material issues (≥ 75%) with minor gaps |
| 40–54 | Identifies some material issues (≥ 50%) with significant gaps |
| 0–39 | Misses most material issues or focuses on non-existent issues |

Note: Credit for defensible alternatives applies here. A reviewer who identifies a genuine issue beyond the reference answers scores in the 85–100 range even if they miss a reference issue of lesser importance.

## Dimension 2: MATERIALITY_ASSESSMENT (0–100)

| Score Range | Criteria |
|-------------|----------|
| 85–100 | Correctly classifies materiality for all identified issues; clear reasoning |
| 70–84 | Mostly correct materiality assessment; minor errors |
| 55–69 | Correct on most issues; some material issues classified as not material or vice versa |
| 40–54 | Significant errors in materiality assessment; inconsistent application |
| 0–39 | Systematic failure to assess materiality correctly |

---

## Dimension 3: EVIDENCE_SOURCE_COMPARISON (0–100)

For exercises with source evidence provided:

| Score Range | Criteria |
|-------------|----------|
| 85–100 | Correctly identifies all discrepancies between document claims and source evidence; evidence cited specifically |
| 70–84 | Identifies most discrepancies; source evidence used appropriately |
| 55–69 | Identifies some discrepancies; some failure to use source evidence where relevant |
| 40–54 | Limited use of source evidence; significant discrepancies missed |
| 0–39 | Source evidence not used or used incorrectly |

For exercises without source evidence: dimension assessed on ability to identify claim-evidence gaps within the document itself.

## Reference Answers: QEX-0001

> **RESTRICTED — Do not share with applicants.**

Principal reference issues (must be identified for a passing score):

1. **Omission — FCA regulatory enquiry not disclosed** (Section: Principal Risks / Risk 1) — The source evidence reveals an active FCA investigation that is not mentioned anywhere in the risk statement. CRITICAL / MATERIAL.

2. **Unsupported claim — Regulatory compliance risk is LOW** (Risk 1 mitigation) — Given the omitted FCA investigation, the LOW residual risk classification is not supported. CRITICAL / MATERIAL.

3. **Unsupported claim — Staff awareness training currency** (Risk 2 mitigation) — Training described as mitigation was last conducted in March 2022. This is 18–21 months before the year end (December 2023). The statement presents this as an active mitigation without noting it is outdated. SIGNIFICANT / MATERIAL.

4. **Unsupported claim — Business continuity plan "effective"** (Risk 6) — The source evidence reveals the 2020 BCP test identified three critical failure points, two of which remain unresolved. The statement presents the plan as effective without noting this. SIGNIFICANT / MATERIAL.

5. **Unsupported claim / Omission — Key person insurance lapsed** (Risk 3) — The source evidence reveals the CEO's key person insurance lapsed in September 2022 and was not renewed. The risk statement does not disclose this. SIGNIFICANT / MATERIAL.

Defensible alternatives (award credit if identified):
- The overall document structure (a self-prepared board statement, not independently audited) as a scope limitation worth noting.
- The revenue decline (12% drop) not fully explained or mitigated in Risk 4 — the three new product lines are unquantified.
- Lack of specificity in residual risk methodology generally.

---

## Assessor Qualitative Assessment Requirements

For each candidate, the assessor must write a narrative that:
1. Identifies which reference issues the candidate found.
2. Notes any defensible alternative findings and how they were credited.
3. Explains the pass/fail decision for each dimension.
4. Identifies specific strengths and weaknesses in the submission.

Minimum length: 200 words per candidate.
