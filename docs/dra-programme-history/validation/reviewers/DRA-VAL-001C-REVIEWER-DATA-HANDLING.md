# DRA-VAL-001C — Reviewer Data Handling Policy

**Status: OPERATIVE** | Effective from 2026-07-27

---

## 1. Minimum Data Collection

The programme collects only the minimum reviewer data necessary to:
- Assess eligibility and qualifications
- Manage conflict of interest
- Conduct consent and confidentiality processes
- Schedule and assign review work
- Attribute contributions in publications (if consented)

Data fields marked as restricted in §4 are never included in public scientific artefacts.

---

## 2. Storage Location

| Data Category | Storage Location | Access Control |
|---------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Contact details (name, email, organisation) | Restricted access system | Programme lead and authorised staff only |
| COI declaration and assessment records | Restricted access system | Programme lead and independent assessors |
| Consent and confidentiality records | Restricted access system | Programme lead only |
| Qualification exercise submissions | Restricted access system | Programme lead and qualification assessors |
| Review submission content | Restricted access system (until publication) | Programme lead and comparison team |
| Reviewer identifiers (DRA-REV-NNNN) | Scientific artefacts (public-facing) | Public |
| Qualification status (by identifier) | Scientific artefacts | Public |
| Domain coverage (by identifier) | Scientific artefacts | Public |
| Attribution (named, if consented) | Published report | Public |

---

## 3. Access Control

- **Programme lead**: full access to all reviewer records.
- **Qualification assessors**: access to exercise submissions and experience evidence for assigned candidates only.
- **Conflict assessors**: access to COI declarations for assigned candidates only.
- **Comparison team (DRA-VAL-001F and beyond)**: access to reviewer identifiers and aggregate qualification data; no access to contact details.
- **Publication team**: access to attribution preferences; no access to contact details beyond what the reviewer has consented to publish.
- **External parties**: no access to any personally identifiable reviewer information without explicit consent.

---

## 4. Public vs Restricted Registry Fields

### Public fields (may appear in scientific artefacts)

- Reviewer identifier (DRA-REV-NNNN)
- Display name (if NAMED attribution is chosen)
- Anonymous flag (if ANONYMOUS)
- Qualification status (general level — QUALIFIED_GENERAL / DOMAIN_SPECIALIST / ADJUDICATOR / CONDITIONALLY_QUALIFIED)
- Qualified domains
- Reviewer category

### Restricted fields (never published)

- Contact email and telephone
- Full legal name (unless explicitly consented for publication)
- Organisation (unless explicitly consented)
- COI declaration detail
- Experience evidence documents
- Consent record detail
- Confidentiality agreement detail
- Qualification exercise submissions and detailed scores
- Availability and scheduling data

---

## 5. Pseudonymisation

Reviewer identifiers (DRA-REV-NNNN) serve as pseudonyms in scientific artefacts. The mapping from identifier to real identity is held only in the restricted access system.

In all published outputs:
- Review findings are attributed to the reviewer identifier, not the name.
- Comparison statistics reference reviewer identifiers.
- Individual finding lists are attributed to identifiers if published.

---

## 6. Retention

| Record Type | Retention Period |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Contact details | Duration of participation + 1 year |
| COI declarations and assessments | 10 years after programme publication |
| Consent and confidentiality records | 10 years after programme publication |
| Qualification records | 10 years after programme publication |
| Review submissions | 10 years after programme publication |
| Audit trail of registry changes | 10 years after programme publication |

After the retention period, records are deleted. Anonymised aggregate analysis outputs are retained indefinitely as scientific data.

---

## 7. Deletion

Personal data deletion requests are handled as follows:
- Requests before review submission freeze: full deletion where technically feasible.
- Requests after review submission freeze but before anonymised aggregate analysis: individual record deletion with programme impact noted.
- Requests after anonymised aggregate analysis: contact details and identifiable information deleted; anonymised contribution cannot be extracted without invalidating scientific conclusions.

Deletion is confirmed in writing within 30 days of the request.

---

## 8. Consent Withdrawal

Consent withdrawal is a subset of deletion rights. See `DRA-VAL-001C-REVIEWER-CONFIDENTIALITY-AND-CONSENT.md` §12 for the full process.

---

## 9. Security Incidents

Any suspected or confirmed security incident affecting reviewer data must be:
1. Reported to the programme lead immediately.
2. Assessed for personal-data-breach notification obligations.
3. Documented in the incident log.
4. Communicated to affected reviewers promptly.

---

## 10. Publication of Reviewer Identities

Reviewer names are published only when:
- The reviewer has chosen NAMED attribution.
- The name matches the verified identity on file.

Reviewers choosing ANONYMOUS appear as "Anonymous Reviewer" or equivalent in publications.

---

## 11. Contact-Data Separation

Contact data (email, telephone) is stored in a system separate from scientific artefacts and reviewer qualification records. The separation ensures that a breach of the scientific data system does not expose contact details, and vice versa.

---

## 12. Qualification-Evidence Handling

Experience evidence documents (CVs, professional profiles, portfolio samples) are:
- Stored in restricted access only.
- Not transmitted outside the qualification process.
- Retained for the qualification record retention period.
- Redacted or anonymised before any external reference if needed for quality assurance.

---

## 13. Applicable Data-Protection Responsibilities

The programme lead is responsible for ensuring that reviewer data is handled in compliance with applicable data-protection law (including GDPR where applicable). The consent agreement at `DRA-VAL-001C-REVIEWER-CONFIDENTIALITY-AND-CONSENT.md` serves as the legal basis for processing where consent is the appropriate basis. Other legitimate-interests processing bases may apply to programme administration.

Reviewers have the right to:
- Access their personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion (subject to retention obligations and the withdrawal limitations)
- Object to processing

Contact the programme lead to exercise any of these rights.
