## Conflict-Risk Pre-Screening

Before investing in formal outreach, prospects should be assessed for obvious conflict risks:
- Known relationship with Runtime Governance Labs or project contributors
- Known involvement in DRA or CTS development
- Organisation with an obvious competitive or financial interest in the outcome

High-conflict-risk prospects should not be contacted unless the programme lead determines that the conflict is likely to be manageable.
