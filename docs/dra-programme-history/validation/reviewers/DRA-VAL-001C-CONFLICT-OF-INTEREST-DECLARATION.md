# DRA-VAL-001C — Conflict-of-Interest Declaration

**Declaration Form Version: DRA-COI-v1.0** | Effective from 2026-07-27

---

## 1. Purpose

This declaration must be completed by every applicant before qualification proceeds. Conflicts are independently assessed — the reviewer's own classification is advisory and may be revised.

A reviewer cannot self-approve their own conflict assessment.

---

## 2. Conflict Categories to Declare

For each category, the declarant must indicate whether a conflict exists, provide details if it does, and classify its severity.

### Category A — Financial Interest in the Project

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| A-1 | Financial interest (equity, debt, options, warrants) in Runtime Governance Labs or any related entity |
| A-2 | Financial interest in Document Release Assurance as a product, licence, or commercial property |
| A-3 | Any other financial arrangement that could be affected by the evaluation outcome |

### Category B — Employment and Advisory Relationships

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| B-1 | Current or recent (within 5 years) employment relationship with Runtime Governance Labs |
| B-2 | Current or past consulting, advisory, partnership, or subcontracting relationship with the project |
| B-3 | Investment, directorship, or governance role in Runtime Governance Labs or a related entity |
| B-4 | Expectation of future employment, consulting, or other relationship with the project |

### Category C — Evaluator and Benchmark Development Involvement

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| C-1 | Involvement in the design, specification, or architecture of CTS or DRA |
| C-2 | Involvement in evaluator implementation, testing, or debugging |
| C-3 | Involvement in benchmark corpus construction or protocol design |
| C-4 | Involvement in the scientific validation protocol design |
| C-5 | Involvement in DRA-001-07 engineering validation |
| C-6 | Any other involvement in the development of the technology under evaluation |

### Category D — Prior Output Access

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| D-1 | Prior access to DRA evaluator outputs or decisions |
| D-2 | Prior access to expected findings for benchmark documents |
| D-3 | Prior access to reviewer submissions from other participants |
| D-4 | Authorship of or contribution to any benchmark corpus document |

### Category E — Personal and Organisational Conflicts

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| E-1 | Close personal relationship (partner, spouse, family member) with founder or known contributors |
| E-2 | Close personal friendship or professional mentoring relationship with founder or known contributors |
| E-3 | Organisational relationship: employer or client has a material interest in the outcome |
| E-4 | Competitive conflict: reviewer or employer competes with Runtime Governance Labs or DRA |

### Category F — Academic and Reputational Conflicts

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| F-1 | Academic thesis, research project, or grant dependent on or affected by the outcome |
| F-2 | Publication or reputation interest in a particular outcome |
| F-3 | Academic or professional rivalry with a party that benefits from a specific outcome |
| F-4 | Public commitments (publications, presentations, blog posts) that would be contradicted by an adverse outcome |

### Category G — Litigation and Regulatory Conflicts

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| G-1 | Active litigation involving Runtime Governance Labs or DRA |
| G-2 | Regulatory investigation involving Runtime Governance Labs or DRA |
| G-3 | Commercial dispute arising from or connected to DRA |

### Category H — Other

| Item | Declaration Required |
|------|---------------------|
| H-1 | Any other circumstance that a reasonable third party might consider to affect the declarant's impartiality |

## 3. Severity Classification

For each conflict identified, the declarant classifies severity:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| **NONE** | No conflict exists in this category |
| **DISCLOSED_NON_MATERIAL** | Potential conflict disclosed but reasonably assessed as non-material (e.g. a tangential published opinion on the topic) |
| **MANAGEABLE** | Material conflict that can be managed with documented restrictions (e.g. assignment to documents in unaffected domains only) |
| **DISQUALIFYING** | Conflict is too material to manage; participation would compromise independence |
| **REQUIRES_INDEPENDENT_ASSESSMENT** | The declarant is uncertain and requests independent assessment |

The independent assessor may revise the declarant's classification.

---

## 4. Required Information for Each Conflict

Where a conflict exists (any category other than NONE), the declarant must provide:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Specific factual description of the conflict |
| **Duration** | How long the relationship or interest has existed |
| **Current status** | Whether it is ongoing or historical |
| **Proposed management** | Suggested mitigation if MANAGEABLE |
| **Supporting documentation** | Reference to any supporting documents where available |

---

## 5. Independent Assessment

After submission, conflicts are assessed by an independent programme officer or external assessor who:
- Was not involved in developing the technology under evaluation
- Has no financial interest in the outcome
- Is not the person whose declaration is being assessed

Assessment outcomes:
- **NONE** — confirmed non-material
- **DISCLOSED_NON_MATERIAL** — confirmed non-material, no restrictions required
- **MANAGEABLE** — restrictions documented and confirmed
- **DISQUALIFYING** — participation not permitted
- **REQUIRES_INDEPENDENT_ASSESSMENT** — escalated to external review

The assessor's decision is binding. The declarant may appeal within 7 days by providing additional evidence.

---

## 6. Assignment Restrictions

Where a conflict is assessed as MANAGEABLE, the assessment record documents specific restrictions, such as:
- Exclusion from specific domains
- Exclusion from specific document types
- Exclusion from adjudicator role
- Pairing requirement (must review alongside an unaffected reviewer)

Assignment systems must enforce restrictions automatically.

---

## 7. Declarant Attestation

The declarant must affirm:

*I confirm that the information provided in this declaration is complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge. I understand that providing materially false information may result in disqualification. I acknowledge that this declaration will be independently assessed and that the assessor's determination is binding, subject to the appeals process.*

This attestation must be recorded as an explicit digital confirmation — it cannot be implied.

---

## 8. Mandatory Review Triggers

Reviewers must re-submit a conflict declaration if any of the following arise after initial qualification:
- Any new financial relationship with Runtime Governance Labs or related entities
- Any new employment or advisory arrangement
- Any new access to evaluator outputs
- Any change in personal relationships covered by Category E
- Any new academic interest in the outcome
- Any new litigation or regulatory matter

Failure to re-declare a new conflict is grounds for disqualification and may invalidate affected review submissions.

---

## 9. Record Retention

COI declarations and assessment records are retained for 10 years after publication of the scientific validation report. Records are held in restricted access storage separate from public scientific artefacts. Reviewer identities are not exposed in public documents.
