## Important Notice

**The Document Release Assurance (DRA) tool is currently under independent scientific evaluation. It has not been proven to be accurate, reliable, or superior to any alternative method. The purpose of this study is to establish whether it performs as intended — the outcome is unknown at the start of the study.**

Participants will be conducting genuine independent assessment. Their findings will be treated as the scientific reference standard. Reviewer independence, honesty, and rigour are essential to the integrity of the study.

---

## 1. What Is This Study?

Runtime Governance Labs is conducting an independent scientific validation of the Document Release Assurance (DRA) tool — a software system designed to identify potential reference issues in documents before publication or official release.

The validation study is structured as a pre-registered, blinded controlled comparison between:
- **Independent human expert reviewers** — people with relevant professional experience who assess documents and identify potential reference issues
- **The DRA evaluator** — the automated tool being evaluated

Human reviewers conduct their assessments independently and without access to the automated tool's output. After all human submissions are frozen, the automated output is unsealed and compared against the human assessments using pre-registered statistical protocols.

This design ensures the study can produce credible scientific evidence — either supporting or failing to support the tool's claimed capabilities.

---

## 2. What Would I Do as a Reviewer?

As an independent reviewer, you would:

1. **Review documents** — read business or professional documents (approximately 10–30 pages each) from a pre-assembled corpus. Documents are synthetic (purpose-created for this study) or are representative samples of professional documents; none contain confidential business information.

2. **Identify potential reference issues** — apply your professional judgement to identify statements in the document that may be inaccurate, unsupported, misleading, or otherwise requiring substantiation or correction.

3. **Record findings independently** — document your findings using a structured form, working independently and without coordinating with other reviewers before submission.

4. **Submit your findings** — submit using the provided format before the submission deadline. After submission, your record is frozen.

5. **Adjudicate disagreements** *(adjudicators only)* — if you are assigned as an adjudicator and two primary reviewers disagree materially, you will be asked to issue a reasoned resolution.

You will **not** have access to the automated tool's output at any point during the review. This blinding is a core requirement of the study design.

---

## 3. What Experience Is Expected?

We are looking for reviewers with genuine professional experience in document review, assurance, or audit contexts. Specific thresholds by role:

**General Assurance Reviewer**
- At least 3 years of professional experience in any relevant assurance domain (compliance, audit, governance, regulatory review, quality assurance, or similar)
- Demonstrated ability to read and critically assess professional documents
- Proficiency in written English

**Domain Specialist**
- At least 5 years of professional experience specifically in one or more of the represented domains (see Section 4)
- Professional qualification, accreditation, or demonstrable seniority in the relevant domain
- Ability to assess documents at practitioner level within that domain

**Adjudicator**
- Must first qualify as a general assurance reviewer
- At least 8 years of professional experience
- Demonstrated competency in assessing competing interpretations and reaching reasoned conclusions

Experience is verified through the qualification process. You will be asked to provide evidence (CV, professional registration, or equivalent) and to complete a brief qualification exercise using training material prepared specifically for this study.

## 6. Independence Requirements

Independent review requires that each reviewer:

- **Works without coordination** — you must not discuss your findings with other reviewers before submitting
- **Reaches your own conclusions** — your assessment must reflect your own independent professional judgement
- **Remains blinded** — you must not access, seek, or accept any information about the automated tool's output before submitting your findings
- **Discloses conflicts** — you must disclose any circumstances that could affect your independence (see Section 7)
- **Does not share documents** — corpus documents are confidential to this study

These independence requirements are not optional. They are the mechanism by which the study produces valid scientific evidence.

---

## 7. Conflict of Interest and Eligibility

Before being assigned to review any document, all reviewers complete a conflict-of-interest declaration. Conflicts are then independently assessed. The following circumstances may disqualify a reviewer or require management:

- Current or prior employment or advisory relationship with Runtime Governance Labs or direct contributors to DRA or CTS
- Financial interest in the success or failure of DRA
- Prior access to DRA evaluator outputs
- Involvement in the design or development of DRA or the benchmark corpus
- Authorship of documents in the corpus
- Close personal or family relationship with programme participants
- Competitive conflict (representing an organisation with a competing product or service)

You do not need to make a final determination yourself — disclose candidly and the programme's conflict assessor will determine whether participation is possible and on what terms.

---

## 8. Confidentiality Requirements

All reviewers are required to accept a confidentiality agreement before qualification or assignment. Key obligations:

- Corpus documents must not be shared with any person not authorised by the programme
- Corpus documents must not be uploaded to external AI or language model systems
- You must not access or attempt to access evaluator output before your submission is complete
- You must not coordinate your findings with other reviewers before independent submission
- The identities of other reviewers are confidential

These obligations remain in force during and after the study, subject to applicable law.

---

## 9. Qualification Process

All reviewers complete a structured qualification process before assignment. The process:

1. **Application** — submit a brief expression of interest with experience summary and domain expertise
2. **Eligibility pre-screen** — your experience is reviewed against the eligibility standard
3. **Conflict declaration** — you complete a conflict-of-interest declaration; it is independently assessed
4. **Consent and confidentiality** — you review and accept the consent and confidentiality agreement
5. **Protocol orientation** — you receive a brief protocol orientation document
6. **Qualification exercise** — you complete a 2–4 hour exercise using purpose-built training material (not corpus documents); the exercise is scored by an independent assessor
7. **Eligibility decision** — an independent assessor makes a pass/fail determination; conditionally qualified outcomes are possible with documented restrictions

Qualification exercises use purpose-built training materials, not documents from the study corpus. Assessors score independently of the reviewer. Credit is given for defensible alternative interpretations.

---

## 12. What This Study Is and Is Not

**This study is:**
- A genuine scientific evaluation of an automated tool
- Designed to produce valid, transparent, and reproducible evidence
- Conducted under a pre-registered protocol with frozen hypotheses
- Dependent on honest, independent expert assessment from qualified reviewers

**This study is not:**
- A validation study with a predetermined outcome
- A promotional exercise for DRA
- An opportunity to provide feedback on or to improve the DRA tool
- A study in which reviewers will learn whether DRA outperformed them or vice versa before the study concludes

Results will be reported transparently regardless of outcome, including outcomes unfavourable to DRA.
