**Document reference:** DRA-VAL-001C-OUTREACH-MESSAGE-PACK  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Audience:** Programme staff conducting outreach — not for distribution to prospects without personalisation  
**Classification:** Internal use; individual messages are public-facing

---

## Purpose and Usage Notes

This pack provides three message templates for initial outreach to prospective reviewers. Each template is a starting point — personalise as appropriate for the specific prospect and channel. Do not alter the substantive accuracy requirements (independence, blinding, conflict disclosure).

**Before sending any message:**
1. Check the prospect against obvious conflict risks (see `DRA-VAL-001C-RECRUITMENT-OUTREACH-TRACKER.md`).
2. Record the prospect in the outreach tracker before contact.
3. Attach or link to `DRA-VAL-001C-EXTERNAL-RECRUITMENT-BRIEF.md`.
4. Do not represent DRA as proven, validated, or superior to any alternative.
5. Invite an expression of interest only — do not imply the prospect has been appointed.

---

## Template A — Professional Contact or Referral

**Use for:** Direct contacts, warm introductions, referrals from known professional networks.

**Suggested channel:** Email or professional message.

---

**Subject:** Independent reviewer opportunity — scientific evaluation study

---

Dear [Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I am reaching out because your professional background — particularly your experience in [domain/area] — makes you a strong potential candidate for an independent reviewer role in a scientific evaluation study we are conducting.

**What the study involves**

We are conducting an independent scientific evaluation of an automated document analysis tool called DRA (Document Release Assurance). The study is designed to determine whether the tool performs as claimed under controlled conditions. The outcome is not predetermined.

As an independent reviewer, you would assess a small number of professional documents using your own professional judgement, identifying statements that may be inaccurate, unsupported, or otherwise require substantiation. Your findings would serve as the independent scientific reference against which the automated tool's output is compared.

This is genuine independent expert assessment. Reviewers are kept blind to the automated tool's output until after all human submissions are frozen.

**Commitment**

The expected commitment is approximately 8–20 hours over a 4–6 week review window. All reviewers complete a short qualification exercise before assignment (approximately 2–4 hours). The qualification uses purpose-built training material, not documents from the study corpus.

**Independence and eligibility**

All reviewers complete a conflict-of-interest declaration before assignment. Independence, blinding, and confidentiality are core requirements. A full description of eligibility criteria, the qualification process, and the commitment involved is attached.

**No commitment at this stage**

This message is simply an invitation to consider participation. If you are interested, please reply with a brief note about your professional background, your relevant domain expertise, and your approximate availability over the coming months. There is no obligation on either side at this stage.

I would be happy to answer any questions you have before you decide.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

With best regards,

## Template C — Independent Adjudicator

**Use for:** Senior practitioners being approached for the adjudicator role.

**Suggested channel:** Email or direct professional approach. Use only for individuals with ≥ 8 years relevant experience and no obvious conflict risks.

---

**Subject:** Independent adjudicator — scientific evaluation study

---

Dear [Name],

I am writing to you because your seniority and experience in [area] suggest you may be well placed to serve as an independent adjudicator in a scientific evaluation study we are conducting.

**The study**

We are evaluating an automated document analysis tool — DRA (Document Release Assurance) — under controlled conditions. The study is structured as a blinded comparison between independent expert reviewers and the automated tool. Reviewers assess professional documents and record their findings independently. After all submissions are frozen, the automated output is unsealed and compared against the human assessments.

The adjudicator role exists to resolve material disagreements between primary reviewers. It is not part of the standard review workflow — it is invoked only when two reviewers differ substantially on whether a specific issue is present or material. The adjudicator reviews both submissions, applies their own professional judgement, and issues a reasoned resolution.

**Eligibility for the adjudicator role**

Adjudicators are required to:
- First complete the general assurance reviewer qualification exercise
- Complete an additional adjudicator qualification exercise (a scenario involving conflicting reviewer submissions, using purpose-built training material)
- Have no prior involvement with either original reviewer submission they are asked to adjudicate

The dual qualification process ensures adjudicators have both the general assurance competency of other reviewers and the specific judgement required to adjudicate fairly.

**Commitment**

Qualification exercises: approximately 4–6 hours (general + adjudicator exercises, conducted once).  
Adjudication assignments: typically 2–3 hours each; only triggered when material disagreement occurs.  
Total expected commitment: 8–16 hours over a 4–6 week period.

**Independence**

Adjudicators are subject to the same conflict-of-interest declaration and blinding requirements as other reviewers. You must not have an employment, advisory, or financial relationship with Runtime Governance Labs or the DRA development team, and must not have prior access to DRA's output.

**No predetermined outcome**

This study is designed to produce credible scientific evidence. The outcome — whether DRA performs as claimed, underperforms, or produces results inconclusive at the sample size available — is not predetermined. Results will be published transparently.

**Expression of interest**

If you are willing to be considered, please reply briefly with your professional background, your area of domain expertise, and your approximate availability over the coming months. Participation begins with eligibility and conflict screening — submitting an expression of interest creates no obligation.

Compensation terms have not yet been finalised and will be communicated before you are asked to commit time to qualification work.

Thank you for your time. I would be glad to answer any questions.

Kind regards,

[Name]  
DRA Validation Programme  
[Contact details]

## Notes on Sending

**Do not:**
- Represent DRA as proven, validated, or commercially ready
- Imply that participation is a formality or that the prospect has already been selected
- Share corpus documents, evaluator internals, or qualification scoring rubrics with prospects before they have accepted confidentiality obligations
- Contact prospects with obvious disqualifying conflicts

**Do:**
- Personalise role and domain references
- Attach the external recruitment brief
- Record the outreach in the tracker before sending
- Follow up within 10 working days if no response
