# DRA-VAL-001C — Reviewer Confidentiality and Consent Agreement

**Agreement Version: DRA-RCA-v1.0** | Effective from 2026-07-27

---

## 1. Study Purpose

The Document Release Assurance Scientific Validation Programme is an independent scientific study investigating whether the DRA automated evaluator produces outputs that align with the findings of qualified human reviewers on real-world governance, assurance, compliance, and policy documents. The study will compare evaluator outputs against an adjudicated human reference standard produced by independent reviewers.

This programme is scientific research. Its purpose is to determine what is true about DRA's performance — including outcomes that may be negative or inconclusive. You are not being asked to produce a particular result.

---

## 2. Voluntary Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time before your review submission is frozen without penalty.

Withdrawal after your review submission is included in the anonymised aggregate analysis may limit the programme's ability to delete your individual contribution, but this limitation is explained at §12.

---

## 3. Permitted Use of Reviewer Contributions

Your review contributions will be used exclusively for:
- The DRA scientific validation study
- Analysis, reporting, and publication of study results
- Methodological documentation of the review process
- Archival of the validation programme record

Your contributions will not be:
- Sold or transferred to third parties
- Used to train or fine-tune AI systems without separate explicit consent
- Used in any commercial context outside the programme

---

## 4. Confidentiality Obligations

By accepting this agreement, you undertake the following obligations.

### 4.1 Document confidentiality

You will not share, distribute, reproduce, or describe the content of benchmark corpus documents outside the programme. Documents are provided solely for the purpose of your review submission.

### 4.2 No unauthorised AI upload

You will not upload benchmark corpus documents or your review findings into any external AI system (including general-purpose language models, code assistants, or document analysis tools) without explicit written authorisation from the programme lead.

### 4.3 No evaluator output access

You will not seek, view, or be told the DRA evaluator's outputs, decisions, or issue lists for any document before submitting your own independent review of that document. Viewing evaluator outputs before submission invalidates the scientific value of your review.

### 4.4 No pre-submission coordination

You will not discuss the content, findings, or implications of any assigned document with any other reviewer before you have submitted your own independent review of that document.

### 4.5 No third-party disclosure

You will not disclose your specific findings on individual benchmark documents to any person outside the programme team before the programme publishes its results.

---

## 5. Handling of Benchmark Materials

- Corpus documents are provided in a secure format specified by the programme.
- Documents must be stored securely and not copied to unprotected locations.
- Documents must be deleted or returned on programme request or on programme completion.
- You must report any suspected unauthorised access to benchmark materials immediately.

---

## 6. Prohibition on Sharing Documents

You must not share benchmark corpus documents with any third party, including colleagues, collaborators, or family members, regardless of purpose.

---

## 7. Prohibition on AI System Upload

You must not upload corpus documents or descriptions of their content into:
- General-purpose AI systems (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, or equivalents)
- Domain-specific AI analysis tools
- AI-assisted writing or review tools

This prohibition applies unless you receive explicit written authorisation from the programme lead.

---

## 8. Prohibition on Seeking Evaluator Output

You must not attempt to obtain DRA evaluator outputs for any document assigned to you before submitting your independent review. This includes requesting outputs from colleagues, programme staff, or any technical system.

---

## 9. Data-Retention Policy

Your review contributions and qualification records will be retained for:
- **10 years** after publication of the scientific validation report

Your contact information and personal details will be retained only for as long as necessary to manage your participation and will be handled under the applicable data-protection responsibilities set out at §17.

---

## 10. Attribution and Anonymity Options

You may choose one of the following attribution options:

| Option | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **NAMED** | Your name appears in the published report's reviewer acknowledgements |
| **ANONYMOUS** | Your contribution is described as an anonymous reviewer |
| **PROGRAMME_DEFAULT** | The programme's standard practice applies (currently: named unless requested otherwise) |

Attribution preference may be changed before final publication.

---

## 11. Publication Treatment

Study results will be published in full, including negative and inconclusive findings. No selective publication. Individual reviewer findings are not published in a way that identifies your specific submissions unless you consent separately.

---

## 12. Withdrawal Rights

You may withdraw participation at any time before your review submission is frozen.

After your submission is included in anonymised aggregate analysis:
- Individual withdrawal is no longer possible without disrupting the scientific record.
- You may request that your identified information is removed from administrative records.
- Anonymised aggregate contributions cannot be withdrawn without invalidating scientific conclusions.

The programme will explain the specific limitations at the time of your withdrawal request.

---

## 13. Compensation

If compensation was agreed as part of your participation, the terms are documented in your participation agreement. Compensation is not results-linked.

---

## 14. Intellectual Property

Your review contributions are provided for the purpose of this programme. You retain your general professional knowledge and methods. The programme holds rights to the specific review submissions for scientific publication.

---

## 15. Data-Protection Notice

Your personal data (name, contact details, professional information, review contributions) is collected and processed for the purpose of this scientific validation study. It is stored securely and accessed only by authorised programme staff. It is not transferred to third parties outside the programme except as required to comply with legal obligations.

You have rights to access, correct, and (subject to the withdrawal limitations at §12) request deletion of your personal data. Contact details for exercising these rights are at §16.

---

## 16. Contact and Escalation

Programme-related concerns, complaints, withdrawal requests, or data-protection enquiries should be directed to the programme lead. Contact details are provided in your onboarding pack.

---

This agreement is valid only when ALL of the following are explicitly confirmed:

| Field | Required Confirmation |
|-------|-----------------------|
| **Core consent** | I consent to the use of my review contributions in the DRA scientific validation study |
| **Data retention** | I consent to my review contributions being retained for 10 years after publication |
| **Obligations acknowledgement** | I have read and understand all confidentiality and conduct obligations in this agreement |
| **No AI upload** | I will not upload benchmark materials into unauthorised AI systems |
| **No evaluator output access** | I will not seek evaluator outputs before submitting my independent review |
| **No pre-submission coordination** | I will not coordinate with other reviewers before submission |
| **No third-party disclosure** | I will not disclose individual document findings before programme publication |
| **No document sharing** | I will not share corpus documents outside the programme |

---

## 18. Agreement Updates

If the programme updates this agreement, you will be notified and asked to accept the updated version before continuing participation. Acceptance of an updated version does not alter rights exercised under the previous version.
